The upgrade menu shows all eight upgrades on one page: 1–8 buy, 9 resets points, and 0 exits. Chat digits 1–8 also buy while the menu is open.

Automatic chat hints

Hints use the CS2 wording with a green prefix and brown command names. For example: [Shroudfire] Type upgrade to learn about them.

Audio preferences

Eight persistent toggles appear on one page: Round Start, Round End, Kill Streak, Starvation, Timeout, Camping, AFK, Menu. Camping is independent of AFK. All categories default ON.

Knife damage and purchases

Knife damage rolls once per hit within the inclusive configured range; reversed bounds are accepted and equal bounds give fixed damage. sf_armitive_blocked_weapons accepts comma-separated or whitespace-separated CS:S names and aliases, case-insensitively, with an optional weapon_ prefix. The default blocks m249; an empty string allows all purchases. Buy menu, console, autobuy and rebuy are covered; pickups and spawn loadouts follow existing rules. Unknown names and CS2-only weapons are reported and ignored.

Starvation

Both the live-round and combat delays must expire. Defaults: 60 seconds after freeze time, 45 seconds without dealing or taking damage, 1 HP each second, then 3 HP each second after 20 seconds of continuous starvation. A 100-HP Invisant starting at 1:00 dies with roughly 0:15 left in a two-minute round. The first tick is immediate. Fractional intervals run each game frame without catch-up damage bursts. Combat resets starvation and acceleration. Solo play and rounds without living Armitive do not cause starvation.
